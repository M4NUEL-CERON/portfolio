# Home (portfolio, single page)

Scope: app/page.tsx, the whole one-page portfolio. Mode: Persuade (the recruiter decides and acts).
Audience/job: a technical recruiter on a 30–60 second screen. They need stack, level, remote/timezone and contact. Engineering leads read the lines second.
Action: Connect on LinkedIn (primary). Email and the CV (EN) are secondary.
Proof: CuteURL (live demo plus two repos), API Performance Monitor (repo), Quantum Developers experience from the CV.
Constraints: English only. Nothing inflated: no "senior", no "expert", no invented metrics. Not generic and not corporate.

## Direction contract
